# P1 Safety Rules

P1 is intentionally low energy, but careless construction can still cause injury, spills, electrical shorts or damaged equipment.

- Use only an open-to-atmosphere gravity reservoir.
- Never connect compressed gas.
- Never use a pump to pressurize the test cell.
- Never exceed 800 mm water head or 8 kPa measured differential pressure.
- Place the wet assembly in secondary containment.
- Keep electronics physically separated from the wet zone.
- Use the 3 A fuse and hard E-stop relay interruption exactly as described.
- Wear eye protection during machining and testing.
- Use nitrile gloves and follow current reagent SDS instructions.
- Stop immediately for polycarbonate cracking/crazing, fixture leakage, sensor wetting, electrical heating, unexpected motion, or interlock faults.
- P1 is not a human-rated, pressure-vessel, underwater, aerospace, marine, defense, industrial or life-support article.
