# Repair-Mechanism Research Programs

AHIS v3 retains four physically distinct repair lanes. They are not interchangeable and none receives structural credit from P1.

## R1 — Fast puncture sealing
Target: secondary polymer/ionomer liner capable of reducing leakage after a small perforation. Required evidence includes controlled perforation geometry, temperature, impact conditions, leak-rate reduction, repeatability and post-event mechanical characterization.

## R2 — Replenishable microvascular composite repair
Target: matrix/interface or delamination repair using separately stored reactive components delivered through an embedded network. Required evidence includes network geometry, reservoir depletion, cure state, repeated damage/heal cycles, Mode-I/Mode-II fracture testing as appropriate, and imaging/NDE of the repaired zone.

## R3 — Vitrimer electrothermal repair
Target: dynamic-covalent composite/bondline repair under a bounded temperature/time cycle. Required evidence includes actual material formulation, thermal history, local temperature uniformity, pre/post fracture or interfacial tests, repeated-cycle degradation and sensor survivability.

## R4 — Engineered SMA/liquid-assisted metal-matrix repair
Target: a specifically engineered metal-matrix system in which shape-memory reinforcement assists crack closure and a compatible low-melting constituent assists filling. This is not generic aluminum self-healing. Required evidence includes alloy/process definition, thermal cycle, crack geometry, metallography, tensile/fatigue recovery and repeatability.

## R5 — Sensing-network restoration
Target: restoration of damaged electrical/sensing pathways as a separate function from structural healing. Evidence must show both material/signal recovery and retained measurement fidelity after damage and repair.

Each program has its own promotion gate. AHIS will not infer success in one lane from another.
