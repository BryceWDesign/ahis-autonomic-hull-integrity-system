# Standards-Oriented Validation Path

AHIS v3 does not claim certification. Where a repair lane advances beyond P1, testing should map to the applicable current standard and article type rather than inventing private metrics.

Examples of relevant methods to investigate with qualified engineers and the current standards editions include:
- ASTM D5528 for Mode-I interlaminar fracture toughness of unidirectional composites;
- ASTM D7905 for Mode-II interlaminar fracture toughness;
- ASTM D7136/D7137 for impact damage and residual compressive strength of composite laminates;
- ASTM D2344 for short-beam strength comparison;
- ASTM E2661 for acoustic-emission examination of aerospace composite panels;
- applicable DNV composite-structure guidance for marine/industrial composite design and fabrication.

Always obtain and use the current controlled standard text from the standards body. AHIS does not redistribute proprietary standard documents.
