# Protection architecture and research programs

R1-R5 remain the inherited sealing, vascular, vitrimer, SMA-metal-matrix and sensing-restoration programs. v4 adds formal R6-R9 records and software screens. `configs/research_programs.json` defines each lane's required measurement categories and test groups. Every physical lane is NOT_RUN and receives zero physical credit.

| Lane | Engineering scope | Executable support | Still requires physical evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R6 | Stress-Adaptive Protective Interphase | Bounded reversible phase with hysteresis | Constitutive response, rate dependence, damage reduction, reversibility and environmental durability |
| R7 | Multilayer impact protection | Layer mass and impact-energy accounting | Ballistic/hypervelocity response, debris behavior and rear-wall integrity |
| R8 | Adaptive structural architecture | Ranking supplied mass/stiffness/utility candidates | Geometry-specific stiffness, impact, fatigue and manufacturability |
| R9 | Resilient diagnostic network | Failed-channel exclusion, anisotropic information-gain advice and held-out checks | Actual damaged network performance, wiring faults, calibration recovery and measurement fidelity |
| R10 | Active charged-particle shield integrity | NOT_IMPLEMENTED | Separate field/particle transport, shielding, cryogenic and fault-validation work |

The proposed stack includes a sacrificial bumper, energy-distribution core, stress-adaptive interphase, structural rear wall, fast-sealing layer, vascular repair network, diagnostic layer and secondary containment. Reference densities/thicknesses are illustrative inputs to a mass screen. No specific material formulation, layer bond qualification or shield performance is claimed.

The biological inspiration is recorded only in design provenance: the discussion of tardigrade survival suggested preserving essential functions through a protective state before attempting repair. Production terminology is Adaptive Survival Envelope and Stress-Adaptive Protective Interphase. This release contains no tardigrade substance, CAHS implementation or demonstrated biological material mechanism.

A `screen_record` success only means a supplied record has the declared categories, limits, IDs and reviewer field needed for human review. It cannot authenticate raw data, qualify hardware, grant physical credit or approve operational use. Program limits must come from an actual test plan, not the synthetic reference scenario. There are no manufactured physical test results in this handoff.

Sensing remains diagnostic. The new path does not create an integrated piezoelectric sensor/actuator fiber mesh that reshapes itself. The legacy modal model is a separate mathematical damping model. This architectural choice is not a patent freedom-to-operate opinion; no non-infringement claim is made.
